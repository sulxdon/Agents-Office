# ArwStyle Website Plan — Bani Yas, Abu Dhabi

**Concept:** Modern men's barbershop & styling salon. Premium look, neighborhood-friendly. Bilingual EN/AR with RTL toggle.

## 1. Branding
**Logo:** ARW monogram in gold square + scissors line. Tagline: Style Redefined | ستايل يليق فيك
**Palette:** Charcoal #1A1A1E (primary), Gold #C9A86A (accent), Off-White #F8F5F0 (bg), Stone Grey #6B7280 (text-muted)
**Fonts:** Poppins (EN headings) + Inter (EN body) / Cairo (AR headings+body) — supports RTL.
**Imagery:** Real shop cuts, fades, beard trims, Bani Yas community. No stock luxury clichés.
**Tone:** Confident, welcoming, local. EN + Gulf Arabic.

## 2. Sitemap
`/` Home | `/services` | `/gallery` | `/reviews` | `/about` | `/contact` + sticky WhatsApp Book button + language switcher EN | عربي
Header nav + footer with hours, phone, map link. Mobile-first.

## 3. Pages & Core Copy (EN / AR)

**HOME:**
Hero: "Bani Yas' Sharpest Cuts. Look Sharp, Feel Confident." / "أحلى حلاقة في بني ياس. أناقة تليق فيك."
Sub: Walk-ins welcome. Fades, beard sculpting, kids cuts from AED 30.
CTAs: [Book on WhatsApp] [View Services & Prices]
Trust strip: 4.8★ Google | 5+ Years in Bani Yas | Expert Barbers
Sections: Popular services (3 cards), Before/After slider, Reviews preview, Location strip with map.

**SERVICES (/services):**
1. Classic Haircut – 40 AED / حلاقة كلاسيك
2. Skin Fade / Taper – 45 AED / حلاقة فيد
3. Beard Trim + Hot Towel – 30 AED / تهذيب اللحية بالمنشفة الساخنة
4. Kids Cut (<12) – 35 AED / حلاقة أطفال
5. Hair Color / Grey Coverage – from 60 AED / صبغة وتغطية الشيب
6. Facial + Cleanup + Head Massage – from 50 AED / تنظيف بشرة ومساج
Each with duration, price, Book link. Note: Prices to confirm with owner.

**GALLERY (/gallery):** 12-16 grid, categories: Fades / Beards / Kids / Shop. Instagram feed embed.

**REVIEWS (/reviews):** Google reviews embed + WhatsApp testimonial form. Prompt: "Had a fresh cut? Leave us a review" / "عجبتك الحلاقة؟ قيّمنا"

**ABOUT (/about):** "Your neighborhood barbers in Bani Yas East, Abu Dhabi. Clean tools, skilled barbers, honest prices." / "صالونك في بني ياس شرق..." Team photos, hygiene promise.

**CONTACT (/contact):** Address: Bani Yas East, Abu Dhabi (embed Google Map), Hours: Daily 10AM-10PM Fri 2PM-10PM, Phone/WhatsApp [to confirm], Click-to-call, WhatsApp booking form (Name/Service/Date), Directions button.

URLs: /en + /ar. Titles: "Barber in Bani Yas, Abu Dhabi | ArwStyle" / "حلاق في بني ياس أبوظبي | أرو ستايل"
Keywords: barber Bani Yas, حلاق بني ياس, men's salon Abu Dhabi, صالون رجالي بني ياس, fade haircut Abu Dhabi
Schema: LocalBusiness/BarberShop with geo, hours, phone. Google Business Profile link, Arabic alt-texts.
